# Zeyoorh.ng Premium Landing Page

## Goal
Create a polished, mobile-first dark landing page for Zeyoorh.ng that presents the brand as an exclusive destination for premium menswear and accessories, while directing visitors to Instagram and WhatsApp.

## Page structure
Build the page in this exact order:
1. Sticky navigation with Zeyoorh.ng, section links, and an outlined enquiry button.
2. Full-height hero with the supplied man-in-white photo, a subtle low-opacity hexagon texture, “Limited Spots Available,” bold positioning copy, and Instagram/WhatsApp actions.
3. “How It Works” with three sequentially revealed steps.
4. Interactive Categories area with Clothing, Shoes, Watches, Native Caps, and Shadda selectors.
5. “What We Offer” with six unboxed icon-and-text benefits.
6. Testimonials shown as a clean staggered quote list.
7. About Us with two concise brand paragraphs.
8. Smooth animated FAQ accordion.
9. Centered final action area with social proof and enquiry action.
10. Minimal footer with brand, copyright, quick links, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

## Visual direction
- Pure `#050505` dark foundation with restrained warm-gold accents and high-contrast ivory text.
- Completely flat composition: no cards, boxed sections, section borders, or shadows.
- Large editorial typography, generous spacing, crisp iconography, and restrained dividers only where needed inside controls such as the FAQ.
- Use the hexagon pattern only behind the hero at very low opacity.
- Maintain strong readability and intentional image crops on both phone and desktop screens.

## Product imagery
- Add the supplied photos through the project’s hosted asset system.
- Hero: man in white traditional attire and native cap.
- Clothing: man in white plus the three fabric-roll images.
- Shoes: the supplied slide photos.
- Native Caps: supplied white and blue caps displayed on gold heads, plus the cap sizing photo where useful.
- Watches and Shadda: keep polished empty states ready for the user’s later photos rather than inventing or reusing unrelated imagery.

## Interactions and motion
- Smooth anchor scrolling from the navigation.
- Reusable fade-and-rise reveals triggered as sections enter view.
- Stagger the three process steps, feature items, and testimonials.
- Cross-fade category descriptions and product imagery when selectors change.
- Smooth FAQ height expansion and chevron rotation.
- Subtle button scale on hover and press.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Content and actions
- Write concise premium copy tailored to Northern Nigerian menswear, accessories, and personal sourcing.
- Provide distinct Instagram and WhatsApp actions without adding a form or backend.
- Use temporary safe link targets until the exact Instagram handle and WhatsApp number are supplied; these remain the only launch details needed later.
- Add unique Zeyoorh.ng page title, description, and social metadata.

## Technical details
- Replace the placeholder home screen with the complete single-page experience.
- Use the existing button and accordion building blocks, semantic color tokens, and Lucide icons.
- Implement viewport-triggered motion with browser-native observation and CSS transitions, avoiding an unnecessary animation dependency.
- Verify category switching, FAQ behavior, navigation, image loading, reduced motion, and layout at mobile and desktop sizes.
